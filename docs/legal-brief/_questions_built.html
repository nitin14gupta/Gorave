Gorave: 10 questions before launch

Gorave Pvt. Ltd. (incorporation in progress) · 21 November 2026 · Pre-launch

What Gorave is

  • An app (Android, iOS) and website for adults (18+) in Indian cities to find and join small social events hosted by other users: house parties, rooftops, game nights, dinners.
  • Hosts verify their PAN, accept a Host Agreement, and list events (free, or paid from ₹99). They can make events women-only or men-only.
  • Guests pay through Razorpay. Gorave charges guests a ₹50 platform fee per ticket and takes a 10% commission from the host's payout. Hosts are paid after the event.
  • Users chat (1:1 and event group chats), share photos, review events, and can report and block. We moderate and can lock accounts.
  • Sign-in is by phone OTP only. We send three kinds of SMS: OTP, "account unlocked", and an SOS alert to the user's chosen emergency contact.

The questions

1What are we, legally?

We host user content and connect hosts with paying guests; we don't organise events.

Need: Are we an intermediary (IT Act / IT Rules 2021), a marketplace e-commerce entity (Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020), or both? What do we have to do as a result (e.g. Grievance Officer, showing host details on listings)?

2Who is liable if something goes wrong at an event?

Hosts are independent. Our terms say the host is responsible for the venue, safety and permissions (alcohol, music licences, police), and limit our liability to what the guest paid us.

Need: Will this hold up? What must the Host Agreement and Terms say? Should we require hosts to declare permissions per event, or carry insurance?

3Is our wallet allowed without an RBI licence?

Refunds land in an in-app wallet. It only holds refunds (no top-ups, no transfers, no cash-out), never expires, can only be spent on Gorave tickets, and a refund can be sent back to the original card/UPI on request.

Need: Confirm this is a closed-system prepaid instrument outside RBI licensing. What must happen to a balance if a user deletes their account?

4Is our money flow compliant?

Guests pay into the company's Razorpay account. We pay hosts after the event (manually now; Razorpay Route later), and can hold a payout while reports are investigated.

Need: Any RBI Payment Aggregator / escrow requirements on our side? Any limit on how long we can hold host money?

5What tax do we collect and charge?

Most hosts are unregistered individuals.

Need: GST on our ₹50 fee and 10% commission (and invoices)? Do we deduct TCS (GST s.52) and TDS (Income Tax s.194-O) from host payouts? Any GST liability for us on the ticket price?

6Are our refund rules fair and enforceable?

Guest cancels 48h+ before: ticket price refunded, ₹50 fee kept. Inside 48h or no-show: no refund (they can pass the ticket to a friend once). Host or Gorave cancels: everything back. Fake event found after the fact: everything back, host not paid.

Need: OK under the Consumer Protection Act? Anything we must change or add?

7Can we run women-only and men-only events?

This is a core safety feature, especially for women. Seats are offered by the gender on the profile.

Need: Any discrimination or other legal risk? How should our Terms describe it?

8What do we need for personal data (DPDP Act)?

We collect phone, name, photos, date of birth, gender, optional location and contacts (contacts only to pick an SOS contact), chats, and for hosts a PAN check (via Surepass: we store the result, masked PAN, a hash, and the name/DOB returned). Locked users who appeal send a live selfie that staff compare by eye.

Need: The consent notice and records we need at sign-up and for optional permissions; what we may keep from the PAN check and for how long; any special rule for the appeal selfie.

9Are our deals and urgency labels allowed?

Hosts can offer early-bird prices (at most 50% off, for at most half the seats) and promo codes (up to 50% off). We show "was ₹1,299, now ₹908" and live counts like "3 left". The ₹50 fee is shown before payment.

Need: Compliant with the CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023?

10Are our agreements validly accepted?

Users accept Terms and Privacy by continuing at sign-up ("By continuing you agree…"). Hosts tick through the Host Agreement and draw a signature with their finger; we store the image and time.

Need: Is that enough (IT Act s.10A)? Should we store which version each person accepted?

Also tell us

  • Anything that could get us rejected by Apple or Google (we have report/block, in-app account deletion, an 18+ rating, and use Razorpay because tickets are for real-world events).
  • Registrations we need: GST, TRAI DLT for SMS, trademark "Gorave", anything else.
  • Who can be our Grievance Officer (must it be a resident founder or employee?).

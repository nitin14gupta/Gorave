# Community Guidelines

**Gorave Pvt. Ltd.** · Last updated: [date]

> DRAFT. These are the rules for everything people post and do on Gorave. Rule 3(1)(b) of India's IT Rules 2021 requires us to publish them, and Apple and Google require them for apps with user content. They apply to profiles, photos, voice intros, chats, event group chats, event listings, reviews, event albums and vibes, and to behaviour at events.

## The idea
Gorave is for meeting new people at real events. That only works if everyone feels safe showing up. Be someone people are glad they met.

## 1. Be real
- One account per person, in your own name, with recent photos of you.
- You must be 18 or over.
- Don't pretend to be someone else, a brand, or Gorave.
- Your gender and age on your profile must be true. Events with a crowd mix rely on them.

## 2. Respect consent, online and offline
- No harassment, bullying, threats or stalking.
- No sexual messages or photos to people who haven't clearly asked for them. Don't keep messaging someone who hasn't replied or has said no.
- No touching without consent at events.
- Blocking is final. Don't try to reach someone who blocked you through another account or through friends.

## 3. Don't post
- Nudity or sexual content, and anything sexual involving minors (we report this to the police).
- Hate or attacks on people for their religion, caste, gender, sexuality, disability, ethnicity or where they're from.
- Violence, threats, or praise for violence or terrorism.
- Illegal drugs, weapons, or selling anything illegal.
- Someone else's private information (phone number, address, photos) without their permission.
- Photos of other people taken or shared without their consent.
- Misinformation that could cause real harm.
- Anything that breaks the law, including copyright: only post photos and music you have the right to use.

## 4. No scams, no spam
- No fake events, bait-and-switch listings, or collecting money outside Gorave for a Gorave event.
- No reselling tickets.
- No advertising, mass messaging, referral spam or crypto/investment pitches.
- No fake reviews, including reviews of your own events.

## 5. Hosts
Hosts also follow the [Host Agreement](host-agreement.md): honest listings, keeping guests safe, and following local laws.

## 6. Reports
- Report a person, message, photo, review or event from the ⋯ menu.
- Report real problems, never to settle a score. False or revenge reports break these guidelines.
- Reports are confidential. We don't tell the person who reported them.

## 7. What we do
Depending on how serious it is and whether it's happened before, we may:
- remove the content,
- warn you (you'll see it in your notifications),
- cancel an event or refund its guests,
- **lock your account**. A locked account is hidden from everyone. You can appeal once from the app, with a short message and a live selfie. If the appeal is turned down, the decision is final.
- report it to the police where the law requires it or someone is in danger.

## 8. If you're in danger
Call **112**. In the app, use **SOS** to alert your emergency contact with your location.

## 9. Questions or complaints
support@gorave.in, or the Grievance Officer (see [Grievance and Contact](grievance.md)).

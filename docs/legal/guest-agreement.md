# Guest Agreement

**Gorave Pvt. Ltd.** · Last updated: [date]

> DRAFT. This applies whenever you join, book or attend an event on Gorave, alongside the [Terms](terms.md), [Community Guidelines](community-guidelines.md) and [Refunds and Cancellation Policy](refunds-cancellation.md).

## In short
- Gorave is the platform. **The host runs the event** and is responsible for the venue and what happens there.
- Your ticket lets you in. It's for you (or one friend you pass it to), and it isn't for resale.
- Bring ID. The host can check your age and turn you away if you don't meet the event's rules.
- Treat people well. Breaking the rules can get you removed from the event and off Gorave.

## 1. Who does what
- **Gorave Pvt. Ltd.** runs the app: listings, booking, payments, chat and safety tools. We're an intermediary under India's IT Act. We don't organise, own or run the events.
- **The host** creates the event and is responsible for it: the venue, permissions, safety at the venue, and that it matches the listing.
- **You** agree to follow this agreement and the host's house rules shown on the event page.

## 2. Joining an event
- **Instant events**: your spot is confirmed when you RSVP or pay.
- **Request-to-join events**: the host decides. You can ask up to twice per event.
- **Crowd mix**: some events keep a set mix of women and men, or are for women only or men only. Spots are offered based on the gender on your profile. Giving a false gender to get a spot breaks this agreement.
- **Waitlist**: if a spot opens, it's offered to the next person on the waitlist, who has 1 hour to take it.
- **Age**: you must be 18 or over to use Gorave. Some events have a higher age limit.

## 3. Your ticket
- Your ticket is a **licence to attend**, not something you own. It can be cancelled under the Refunds and Cancellation Policy.
- You can pass it **once** to another Gorave user. You can't sell it on, including for a markup or outside Gorave.
- The exact address may only be shown to confirmed guests, close to the event. Don't share it publicly.

## 4. At the door
- Check-in is by the QR code in the app. Have it ready.
- Carry a government photo ID. The host may check your age and that the ticket is yours.
- The host can refuse entry or ask you to leave if you break their house rules, this agreement or the Community Guidelines, or are too intoxicated to be safe. In those cases there's no refund.

## 5. How to behave
- Respect everyone's consent and space. No harassment, touching without consent, following people, or unwanted advances, at the event or afterwards in chat.
- No illegal drugs, weapons, or anything illegal at the venue.
- Drink responsibly. Follow local laws on alcohol, including the legal drinking age in that state.
- Look after the venue. You may have to pay for damage you cause.

## 6. Photos and the event album
- Events can have a shared album. Only add photos of people who are fine with it, and remove one if someone asks.
- The host or other guests may take photos. If you don't want to be photographed, say so to the host.
- You can report any photo of you from the album.

## 7. Group chat
- Confirmed guests join the event's group chat. What you post there is seen by the host and other guests.
- The host can pin messages, mute the chat or remove people.
- Blocking someone hides them from you everywhere, including the group chat.

## 8. Safety
- Use **SOS** in the app if you feel unsafe. It alerts your emergency contact with your location.
- In an emergency, call **112** first.
- Report a person, a message, a photo or an event from the app. We review reports and may warn, lock or remove accounts and events.

## 9. Your risk and our liability
- You take part in events at your own risk and are responsible for your own conduct, belongings and travel.
- Gorave isn't liable for what happens at an event, the venue, or the behaviour of hosts and other guests, except where the law says we can't exclude it.
- Where we are liable, our total liability for an event is limited to what you paid us for that event (ticket plus platform fee).

## 10. If something goes wrong
Report it from the event page, or email support@gorave.in. For complaints about content or our decisions, contact the Grievance Officer (see [Grievance and Contact](grievance.md)).

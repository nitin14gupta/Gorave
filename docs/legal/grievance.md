You can complain to the Grievance Officer about content on Gorave, a decision we made about your account or content, or how we handle your personal data.

**What happens next**
- We acknowledge your complaint within **24 hours**.
- We resolve it within **15 days**.
- If the content is a sexual image of you, or shows you in a way that violates your privacy, we act on it within **24 hours** of your complaint.

Please include your registered phone number, links or screenshots of what you're complaining about, and what you'd like us to do.

## Personal data (DPDP Act 2023)
For requests about your data (access, correction, deletion, or withdrawing consent), email grievance@gorave.in. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## Requests from police and authorities
Lawful requests from government and law enforcement go to legal@gorave.in. We respond as the law requires.

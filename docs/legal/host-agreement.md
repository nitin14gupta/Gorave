## In short
- **It's your event.** You run it and you're responsible for it. Gorave brings the people and handles booking and payments.
- Verify your ID before hosting, and describe your event honestly.
- Get whatever permissions your venue and city need.
- You're paid the ticket price minus a 10% commission after the event. Guests pay the ₹50 platform fee on top.
- If guests report an event as fake or misleading and we agree, they're refunded and you aren't paid.

## 1. Who can host
- You must be 18 or over and have a Gorave account in your own name.
- Before your first event you complete the **host ID check**: your PAN is verified with a government-backed check, and your name and date of birth must match. A Gorave admin approves it. One PAN can only be used for one account. You get two tries; after that, contact support.
- You add a **payout method** (UPI or bank account) in your own name.

## 2. Creating an event
- Describe the event honestly: what it is, where, when, the price, the crowd and the house rules. Photos must be of your event or venue, or clearly representative of it.
- Paid tickets are at least ₹99. Free events are limited to 2 per month per host.
- You choose instant join or request-to-join, the capacity, an age limit, and optionally a crowd mix (a set share of women and men, women only, or men only).
- The exact address can be kept for confirmed guests.
- You can edit details up to **7 hours** before the start, and capacity up to **2 hours** before.

## 3. Money

| | |
|---|---|
| Guest pays | Your ticket price + Gorave's ₹50 platform fee per ticket |
| Gorave keeps | The platform fee, plus a **10% commission** on your ticket price |
| You receive | Ticket price minus 10%, for every ticket that isn't refunded |
| When | After the event ends, within **[7] business days**, if there are no open reports about it **[DECIDE]** |
| How | To your verified UPI or bank account in your name |
| Taxes | You're responsible for your own income tax and GST. Gorave may deduct tax at source where the law requires it (for example TCS under GST and TDS under section 194-O) and show it on your payout statement **[CA to confirm]**. |

**Payouts can be held** while we look into reports about an event. If we find the event was fake, misleading, or didn't happen as listed, we refund the guests in full and you aren't paid for that event. If we've already paid you, we may recover the amount from your future payouts.

### Deals
You can offer an **early-bird** price (the first tickets for less) and **promo codes**. To keep your full price honest, an early bird is at least half the full price for at most half your seats, and a code is up to 50% off; deeper codes (up to 100%) are only for guest lists of 10 uses or fewer. Guests get the cheaper deal, never two. **You fund the discount**: your payout is on the price each guest actually paid.

## 4. Cancelling and changing your event
- You can cancel up to **48 hours** before the start, from the event page. Every guest gets everything back, ticket and platform fee. Closer to the start, contact Gorave.
- If you move the date by more than 7 days, guests who bought before the change get 48 hours to cancel for a full refund.
- Repeated cancellations or big changes can lead to warnings or losing hosting access.

## 5. Your responsibilities at the event
- **You run the night.** Keep your guests safe and step in if something feels off. Have a way to contact you on the night.
- **Zero tolerance for harassment**, from you or anyone at your event. Remove people who harass others.
- **Follow local laws**, and get what your event needs. This can include: venue permission, police permission for public events, an excise licence where alcohol is served or sold, music licences (for example PPL or IPRS) for playing recorded music in public, fire safety and capacity limits, and noise rules. Gorave doesn't get these for you.
- **Age**: check ID at the door. Keep to the event's age limit and the legal drinking age in your state.
- **Check-in**: scan guests' QR codes in the app.
- **Group chat**: you can pin messages, mute the chat and remove people. Use these fairly.

## 6. Reports
- Report real problems: a guest who harassed someone, broke the law, or was dangerous. False or revenge reports break this agreement.
- Guests can report you and your event. We review reports and may warn you, cancel an upcoming event, refund guests, hold your payout, or lock your account.

## 7. It's on you, not just Gorave
- You're an independent organiser, not an employee, agent or partner of Gorave.
- You're responsible for your event, your venue, and any loss, injury or damage caused by you, your staff or your venue. Consider event insurance.
- You agree to cover Gorave's losses (including legal costs and fines) that come from your event, your breach of this agreement, or your breaking the law.
- Gorave isn't liable for what happens at your event, except where the law says we can't exclude it.

## 8. Ending hosting
- You can stop hosting any time. Events you've already listed must be run or cancelled under section 4.
- We can suspend or end your hosting access if you break this agreement, the Terms or the Community Guidelines, or if we're required to by law. If your account is locked, you can appeal once from the app.
- Sections 3 (for money still owed either way), 7 and 9 continue after hosting ends.
